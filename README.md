# Whispers for the Dead

Genre: First-Person Zombie Shooter
Platform: PC 
Setting: Post-apocalyptic urban and rural landscapes

In Whispers of the Dead, players are thrust into a chilling post-apocalyptic world overrun by the undead. Set in the aftermath of a mysterious pandemic that turned much of the population into zombies, the game’s eerie atmosphere is defined by unsettling silence, punctuated by distant, ominous whispers. These whispers, coming from the undead themselves, are remnants of the humans they once were, creating an emotional and psychological horror alongside the physical threat.

Story:
The year is 2074. After the "Silent Plague", humanity is on the brink of extinction. Those who remain must not only survive the ravaging hordes of zombies but also uncover the dark secret behind the origin of the outbreak. The whispers you hear are said to be more than just mindless noise; they hold the key to humanity's survival.

You play as Ethan Summers, a former soldier and one of the few survivors capable of understanding the whispers, which provide cryptic clues that guide him through a harrowing journey. Alongside a small group of survivors, Ethan must traverse a vast world of urban ruins, decaying farmlands, and eerie forests, scavenging for supplies and fighting off ever-evolving zombies that grow more dangerous with each encounter.

Visual and Audio Style:
Whispers of the Dead employs a dark, gritty aesthetic, with muted colors that reflect the bleakness of the world. The sound design is key to the immersion, with minimal music to heighten tension. The unsettling whispers are a core element, guiding players through the game and adding layers of psychological horror. Ambient sounds, such as distant moans, creaking wood, and footsteps, keep players on edge throughout their journey.

Tagline:
"The dead don’t rest... they whisper."
